# Service Level Agreement

Business tier customers are guaranteed 99.9% monthly uptime. Enterprise tier
customers are guaranteed 99.95% monthly uptime. If Nimbus fails to meet the
guaranteed uptime in a calendar month, affected customers receive service credits
according to the following schedule: below 99.9% but above 99.0%, a 10% credit;
below 99.0%, a 25% credit. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the
incident via a support ticket and are applied to the following month's invoice.
Starter and Team tiers do not carry an uptime SLA.
