# Pricing Tiers

Nimbus Cloud Storage offers four tiers:
- Starter: $9/month, 100GB storage, 1 user, community support.
- Team: $29/month per user, 1TB pooled storage, priority email support.
- Business: $79/month per user, 5TB pooled storage, SSO, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, unlimited storage, dedicated account manager, and a
  99.95% uptime SLA with custom contract terms.

All tiers include end-to-end encryption at rest and in transit, and unlimited
file version history for 30 days.
