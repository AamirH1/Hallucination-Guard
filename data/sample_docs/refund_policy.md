# Refund Policy

Nimbus Cloud Storage offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid subscription
plans (Starter, Team, and Business). Refund requests must be submitted through the
billing portal within 30 calendar days of the original charge. Refunds are issued
to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval. Annual plans
canceled after the 30-day window are not eligible for a prorated refund; monthly
plans can be canceled at any time but are not refunded for partial months.
