# Compliance Certifications

Nimbus Cloud Storage holds SOC 2 Type II certification (renewed annually) and is
ISO 27001 certified as of last year's audit cycle. Nimbus is not currently HIPAA
compliant and does not offer a Business Associate Agreement (BAA); customers with
healthcare data (PHI) should not store it on Nimbus until HIPAA compliance is
announced. Nimbus is GDPR-compliant for EEA customers as described in the GDPR
and Data Privacy document.
