# Onboarding Guide

New Team and Business accounts are assigned an onboarding specialist who
schedules a kickoff call within 3 business days of signup. The onboarding
checklist includes: workspace setup, SSO configuration (Business/Enterprise
only), user provisioning, and a data migration plan if moving from another
provider. Most customers complete onboarding within 2 weeks. Enterprise
onboarding is customized and typically takes 4-6 weeks depending on migration
volume and integration requirements.
