# Enterprise Refund Terms

Enterprise customers on a signed Master Services Agreement (MSA) are subject to the
refund terms in their contract, not the standard 30-day policy. Most Nimbus
Enterprise contracts specify a 14-day refund window from the contract effective
date, after which all fees are non-refundable regardless of usage. This is a
deliberate deviation from the standard consumer/SMB refund policy and has caused
confusion among sales teams; contracts always take precedence over the public
refund policy page.
