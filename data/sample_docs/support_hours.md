# Support Hours and Channels

Starter tier: community forum support only, best-effort response time.
Team tier: email support, Monday-Friday 9am-6pm Eastern Time, target first
response within 24 hours.
Business tier: priority email and live chat, 24/5 (Monday-Friday), target first
response within 4 hours.
Enterprise tier: 24/7 phone, email, and chat support with a dedicated Slack
channel and a named customer success manager.
