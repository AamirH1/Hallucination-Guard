# Incident Response Policy

Nimbus classifies incidents into three severities: SEV1 (full outage or data
breach), SEV2 (partial degradation affecting a subset of customers), and SEV3
(minor, non-customer-facing issue). SEV1 incidents trigger a public status-page
update within 15 minutes and a customer notification email within 1 hour.
Post-incident reviews for SEV1 and SEV2 incidents are published within 5
business days on the Nimbus trust portal.
