# GDPR and Data Privacy

Nimbus Cloud Storage acts as a data processor under GDPR for customers based in
the European Economic Area. Customers can request a full export of their data or
a full deletion ("right to erasure") by contacting privacy@nimbuscloud.example.
Data subject access requests are fulfilled within 30 days as required by GDPR
Article 12. Nimbus does not sell customer data to third parties under any
circumstances.
